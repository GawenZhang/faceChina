Shanghai Baobang Medical Equipment Co., Ltd.

We publish the MACY-PAN hyperbaric chamber pages and the O2Magic companion app. This site lists the HE5000 models, photographs and drawings so you can check the body and the attached equipment.

Product range

The models introduced here are HE5000 Mono, HE5000 Standard, HE5000 PLUS, HE5000 Fort and HE5000 Diamond. The drawings use the brand MACY-PAN, the contact email xuyun@macy-pan.com and the brand site www.macy-pan.com.

The brand site also lists cryotherapy chambers, hard hyperbaric chambers, lying portable chambers, sitting portable chambers, stretcher wheel-in chambers and veterinary chambers. The lines it marks as current focuses are the lying portable chamber, the hard chamber and the multiplace chamber. The detail pages on this site cover the HE5000 series.

Millimetre figures on the pages only repeat numbers already printed on the drawings. Pressure range, oxygen concentration and medical registration do not appear on those drawings, so this site does not add them.
