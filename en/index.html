Questions

Which chambers does the site cover?

HE5000 Mono, Standard, PLUS, Fort and Diamond. Occupancy is stated only when the product image states it: Mono is a single-person chamber, Standard is a 2-person seated chamber, PLUS is a 4-person seated chamber, and Diamond is a single-person rest chamber. The Fort image does not state occupancy.

Which dimensions are listed?

See each model’s length, width and height in millimetres.

What is O2Magic?

O2Magic is the companion app for a home chamber. It shows chamber status and keeps the record on the phone. Results are for everyday self-reference and are not a diagnosis, treatment or medication advice.
