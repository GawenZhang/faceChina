O2Magic

O2Magic is the companion app for a home chamber. It shows chamber status on the phone, connects on the same network, and keeps the session record on that phone. After you grant the camera, you can run a short face scan and see a heart-rate reference.

These results are for everyday self-reference. They are not a diagnosis, treatment or medication advice from a medical institution.

Download the Android package

Chamber connection

Find and connect the chamber on home Wi-Fi and view oxygen concentration, chamber pressure and remaining time. When Android scans wireless networks it may ask for location permission. That permission is not used to track where you are.

Local record

Chamber readings and history stay on your phone by default. Save any file you need before you uninstall the app.

Face reference

After you start a scan and allow the camera, the app runs a short scan. You can turn the camera permission off in system settings at any time.

Package date 2026-11-06. If the system warns about an unknown source, allow the current browser to install the app. Download it from this site.
