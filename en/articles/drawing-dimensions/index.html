Why the drawing is the size reference

2026-11-12

Every millimetre figure on this site is copied from that model's dimension drawing. The product page links the original PDF. A number from one model is not reused for another.

The Standard drawing marks the body at 2070 × 1749.5 × 1616 mm. The oxygen generator is 714.75 × 756.50 × 471 mm, the air compressor is 1263.85 × 631 × 320 mm, and the air cooler is 588.97 × 604 × 387 mm.

On the Mono drawing the body is 1730 × 1880 × 960 mm. The oxygen generator is (730 ± 10) × (980 ± 10) × 420 mm. The air cooler is 588.97 × 604 × 387 mm. PLUS, Fort and Diamond each have their own drawing. Open that model's page for its marks.

What to check before installation

  1. Measure the clear opening at doors, lifts and turns along the delivery route. Request the packed dimensions and weight before checking that route.
  2. Plan the positions of the chamber and associated equipment, door operation and service access. Use the drawing for initial planning and the installation instructions to confirm the required clearances.
  3. Confirm power, ventilation, floor loading and on-site installation requirements with the supplier. Keep the drawing and instructions that match the ordered configuration.
