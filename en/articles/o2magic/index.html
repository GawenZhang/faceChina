What O2Magic is for

2026-11-12

O2Magic is the companion app for a home chamber. It shows chamber status and keeps the use record on the phone. The Android package on this site is dated 2026-11-06.

A reading, or a short face scan you start yourself, is for everyday self-reference. It does not replace a diagnosis, treatment or medication advice from a medical institution.

Chamber readings stay on the phone by default.
