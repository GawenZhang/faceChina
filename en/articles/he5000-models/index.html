How the five HE5000 models differ

2026-11-12

The HE5000 series on this site has five models. Occupancy is written only when the product image states it. Mono is a single-person chamber. Standard is a 2-person seated chamber. PLUS is a 4-person seated chamber. Diamond is a single-person rest chamber. The Fort product image does not state how many people it holds.

Mono has a side door, with the touchscreen, pressure gauge and emergency button on the front. Standard is a black chamber with a diamond-quilted interior and two seats. PLUS is a black chamber with a star ceiling and four seats. Fort is a white rest chamber with double doors. Diamond is a white rest chamber with a round door and a lounger, and its drawing says the body is joined from two sections.

This site does not state a pressure range, an oxygen concentration or a medical registration number. Those figures are not printed on the drawings we publish.
