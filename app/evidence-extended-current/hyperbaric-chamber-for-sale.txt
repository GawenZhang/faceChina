L107: ## What to Look for When Buying a Hyperbaric Chamber
L108: 
L109: Not all hyperbaric chambers are created equal. Whether you are purchasing for home use or a clinical facility, here are the essential factors to evaluate before making your decision. Avoid imported or uncertified chambers that may lack proper safety certifications and regulatory clearances.
L110: ### FDA 510(k) Clearance
L111: 
L112: Confirm that the chamber is FDA 510(k) cleared as a Class II medical device. FDA 510(k) clearance means the FDA has reviewed the device and found it substantially equivalent to a legally marketed device. Note that "FDA registered" is not the same as FDA cleared. All OxyHealth chambers carry FDA 510(k) clearance.
L113: ### ASME PVHO-1 Compliance
L114: 
L115: The ASME PVHO-1 standard governs the design and manufacture of pressure vessels for human occupancy. This is the gold standard for hyperbaric chamber safety. Ask any manufacturer for their ASME PVHO-1 GR-1 form as documentation. OxyHealth chambers are built to this standard.
L116: ### ISO 13485 Manufacturing
L117: 
L118: ISO 13485 is the international quality management standard for medical device manufacturing. This certification ensures consistent quality, traceability, and regulatory compliance throughout the production process.
L119: 
L120: ### UL Listed Components
L121: 
L122: Electrical components should be UL (Underwriters Laboratories) listed, confirming they have been independently tested for safety. OxyHealth chambers use UL-listed electrical components throughout.
L123: ### Manufacturer Warranty
L124: 
L125: A comprehensive manufacturer warranty protects your investment and ensures access to genuine parts and factory-trained support. Purchasing from an unauthorized source may void the manufacturer warranty entirely.

L161: ### Why FDA 510(k) Clearance Matters When Buying a Hyperbaric Chamber
L162: 
L163: When shopping for a hyperbaric chamber, be aware that many sellers offer chambers that are only FDA registered, not FDA 510(k) cleared. FDA 510(k) clearance means the FDA has reviewed the device and found it substantially equivalent to a legally marketed device. FDA registration simply means a manufacturer has notified the FDA that they exist. OxyHealth chambers are FDA 510(k) cleared as Class II medical devices, not just registered.
L164: Before purchasing from any seller, always ask for the FDA 510(k) clearance letter and ASME PVHO-1 GR-1 form with serial number. Buying direct from OxyHealth guarantees you receive a genuine, fully certified chamber with valid documentation.
