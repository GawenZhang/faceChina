L67: Critical Distinction
L68: ## FDA Cleared vs FDA Registered
L69: 
L70: Understanding the difference between FDA 510(k) clearance and FDA registration is one of the most important factors when purchasing a hyperbaric chamber. These terms are often confused, but they represent very different levels of regulatory review and consumer protection.
L71: ### FDA 510(k)-Cleared
L72: 
L73:   * Device has been evaluated by the FDA for safety and efficacy
L74:   * Requires substantial evidence of equivalence to a legally marketed device
L75:   * Manufacturer must demonstrate the device meets safety standards
L76:   * Required before the device can be legally marketed in the U.S.
L77:   * All OxyHealth chambers are FDA 510(k)-cleared
L78: ### FDA Registered Only
L79: 
L80:   * Company has only notified the FDA that it exists
L81:   * No review of the device for safety or efficacy
L82:   * Does not mean the device is approved or cleared
L83:   * Any company can register by filling out a form
L84:   * Not equivalent to clearance in any way
L85: 
L86: Always ask for the FDA 510(k) clearance letter and ASME PVHO-1 GR-1 form.
L87: These documents are the proof that a chamber has been properly evaluated and certified. If a manufacturer cannot provide them, proceed with caution. Learn more on our 【12†FDA documentation page】 and 【13†safety certifications page】.

L169:
