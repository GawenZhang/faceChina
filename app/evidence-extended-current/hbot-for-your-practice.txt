L33: ## The science of breathing under pressure
L34: 
L35: Hyperbaric therapy involves breathing under increased atmospheric pressure, allowing more oxygen to dissolve into the blood plasma and increasing oxygen availability throughout the body.
L36: 
L37: Research continues to investigate the physiological effects of hyperbaric environments, including their influence on:
L38: 
L39:   * Inflammation
L40:   * Cellular signaling
L41:   * Tissue oxygenation
L42:

L234: 
L235: ### What is hyperbaric therapy?
L236: 
L237: +
L238: 
L239: Hyperbaric therapy means breathing in an environment pressurized above normal atmospheric pressure. Under increased pressure, more oxygen dissolves into the blood plasma, which increases oxygen availability throughout the body. Pressure and the oxygen concentration being breathed are separate variables, and protocols are set by a qualified clinician.
L240: ### What does FDA 510(k) clearance mean for a hyperbaric chamber?
L241: 
L242: +
L243: Hyperbaric chambers are Class II medical devices (Product Code CBF, 21 CFR 868.5470). 510(k) clearance means the FDA reviewed the device through its premarket notification process before it could be marketed. Being FDA registered or listed only means a manufacturer or device has been recorded with the FDA, and it does not mean the device is FDA cleared. Verify clearance for the specific chamber model. OxyHealth chambers are 510(k) cleared, not just registered.
