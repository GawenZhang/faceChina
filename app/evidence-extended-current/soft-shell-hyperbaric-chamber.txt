L8: Understanding Portable HBOT
L9: ## What Is a Soft Shell Hyperbaric Chamber?
L10: A soft shell hyperbaric chamber is a portable, inflatable device that delivers hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) by surrounding the user in a pressurized environment. These chambers are constructed from durable, medical-grade flexible materials and are designed to be set up and operated in a home, office, or wellness facility.
L11: Unlike the rigid, permanently installed hard shell chambers found in hospitals and wound care centers, soft shell chambers offer the convenience of portability while still delivering clinically meaningful increases in atmospheric pressure.
L12: Soft shell hyperbaric chambers typically operate at 1.3 ATA (atmospheres absolute), which represents a 30% increase in atmospheric pressure above normal sea-level conditions. Based on Henry's Law of physics, this elevated pressure causes significantly more oxygen to dissolve directly into blood plasma, cerebrospinal fluid, and other body fluids.
L13: This dissolved oxygen can reach tissues that may not be easily accessed by oxygen carried on red blood cells alone, particularly in areas with compromised blood flow.
L14: The term "soft shell" refers to the flexible, fabric-based construction of these chambers, as opposed to the rigid acrylic or steel construction of clinical hard shell chambers. Despite their flexible design, OxyHealth soft shell chambers are engineered to exacting standards and are built to the ASME PVHO-1 pressure vessel standard for human occupancy. They use an external compressor to inflate the chamber, maintain consistent pressure, and circulate fresh air throughout each session.
L15: OxyHealth is the global leader in portable hyperbaric chamber manufacturing, with over 19,000 chambers delivered worldwide. The company's soft shell chambers, including the Vitaeris 320 and Respiro 270, are FDA 510(k) cleared as Class II medical devices and are trusted by physicians, athletes, and families around the world for safe, effective mild hyperbaric therapy.
