L11: # Hyperbaric Chamber Room Size and Setup Requirements
L12: 
L13: A portable OxyHealth chamber needs a clear floor area a little larger than the chamber, between 88 and 100 inches long depending on the model, plus space for the compressor and a 110-120V outlet. Clinical hard-sided chambers weigh 1,250 to 1,400 lbs and are installed by OxyHealth certified technicians.

L24: ## Planning a room for a portable chamber
L25: 
L26: Portable soft-sided chambers such as the Respiro 270, Vitaeris 320, and Quamvis 320 are used in homes and smaller clinics. When you measure, allow for:
L27:   * The inflated chamber. Use the diameter and length above as the minimum footprint.
L28:   * Getting in and out. Leave clear floor beside the entry so the user can enter and exit without climbing over furniture.
L29:   * The compressor. Each system includes a CleanAir compressor that sits outside the chamber, so leave floor space for it next to the chamber.
L30:   * Power. A 110-120V outlet within reach of the compressor.
L31:   * A helper, if needed. Follow the instructions for use on whether a second person should be present.
L32: The Quamvis 320 adds a rigid external steel frame, so plan for the frame as well as the chamber. Portable chambers can be deflated and packed if the room is needed for something else.
L33: ## Planning a room for a clinical hard-sided chamber
L34: 
L35: The Fortius 420 and Fortius 420 EXP are steel pressure vessels that operate up to 4.0 ATA. Planning for them is a facilities question as much as a floor-space question:
L36:   * Weight. 1,250 lbs for the Fortius 420 and 1,400 lbs for the Fortius 420 EXP. Confirm floor loading with your building.
L37:   * Delivery path. Measure doorways, corridors, and elevators. The Fortius 420 EXP uses a split design for sites with limited access.
L38:   * Installation. OxyHealth certified technicians complete installation, assembly, and calibration, and provide training.
L39:   * Facility safety. Hyperbaric safety also involves facility and fire-safety controls addressed by NFPA 99. Requirements depend on your jurisdiction and facility type, so review them with your local authority and safety officer.
L40: See 【12†Fortius 420 vs Fortius 420 EXP】 for how the two clinical models differ.
L41: ## Safety in the room
L42: 
L43: Follow the chamber's instructions for use and your facility's safety policy regarding ignition sources and electronic devices. Keep unauthorized electronics and battery-powered devices out of the chamber, and do not improvise exceptions. The 【13†safety page】 covers the essentials for every OxyHealth chamber.

L45: ## Room and Setup FAQ
L46: ### How much space do you need for a home hyperbaric chamber?
L47: 
L48: Start with the chamber itself. OxyHealth portable chambers range from 27 inches in diameter and 88 inches long (Respiro 270) to 32 inches in diameter and 100 inches long (Quamvis 320). Add room to get in and out comfortably and to place the compressor beside the chamber.
L49: ### What power does a hyperbaric chamber need?
L50: 
L51: OxyHealth chamber systems list a 110-120V power requirement. Confirm the outlet and circuit you plan to use with OxyHealth before delivery.
L52: 
L53: ### How heavy is a clinical hard-sided hyperbaric chamber?
L54: 
L55: The Fortius 420 weighs 1,250 lbs and the Fortius 420 EXP weighs 1,400 lbs. Confirm floor loading and the delivery path with OxyHealth and your building before ordering.
L56: ### Can a portable hyperbaric chamber be moved?
L57: 
L58: Yes. Portable soft-sided chambers can be deflated, packed, and transported. The Respiro 270 packs into a carrying bag.
