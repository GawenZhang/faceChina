L26: ## Where they differ
L27: 
L28: ### Frame
L29: 
L30: The Quamvis 320 has a rigid external steel frame with the SafeSet buckling system and a reinforced ballistic nylon jacket. The Vitaeris 320 is a frameless soft-sided chamber.
L31: 
L32: ### Length
L33: 
L34: The Quamvis 320 is 100 inches long. The Vitaeris 320 is 90 inches long. Interior diameter is the same at 32 inches.
L35: 
L36: ### Closure
L37:

L39: ### What is the same
L40: 
L41: Both operate at 1.3 ATA (4 psi), use a double-sided 44 oz. urethane-coated polyester bladder, include a 1/3 hp CleanAir compressor with a cooling radiator, and run on 110-120V power.

L47: ## Vitaeris 320 vs Quamvis 320 FAQ
L48: 
L49: ### What is the difference between the Vitaeris 320 and the Quamvis 320?
L50: 
L51: The Quamvis 320 is 10 inches longer (100 inches against 90 inches) and has a rigid external steel frame with a reinforced ballistic nylon jacket. Both have a 32 inch interior and operate at 1.3 ATA (4 psi).
L52: ### Is the Quamvis 320 a hard-sided chamber?
L53: 
L54: No. The Quamvis 320 is a portable chamber with a soft bladder supported by a rigid external steel frame. It operates at 1.3 ATA, the same as the Vitaeris 320.

L56: ### Do both chambers use the same compressor?
