L8: # Vitaeris 320 vs Respiro 270: How These Hyperbaric Chambers Compare
L9:

L26: ## Where they differ
L27: 
L28: ### Interior space
L29: 
L30: The Vitaeris 320 is 5 inches wider inside (32 inches against 27 inches) and 2 inches longer. That extra diameter is the main thing users notice.
L31: 
L32: ### Compressor
L33: 
L34: The Vitaeris 320 includes a 1/3 hp CleanAir compressor with a cooling radiator. The Respiro 270 includes a 1/4 hp CleanAir compressor. Both are double-headed and oil-less.
L35: ### Footprint and portability
L36: 
L37: The Respiro 270 is the most compact OxyHealth chamber and packs into a carrying bag. It suits rooms where floor space is limited.
L38: 
L39: ### What is the same
L40: 
L41: Both operate at 1.3 ATA (4 psi), use a double-sided 44 oz. urethane-coated polyester bladder, close with patented 2-zipper seal assemblies, and run on 110-120V power.
L42: ## Which setting each one suits
L43: 
L44:   * Vitaeris 320: Users who want the most interior room in a portable chamber and have the floor space for it.
L45:   * Respiro 270: Smaller rooms, smaller clinics, and users who expect to move or store the chamber.
L46: Chamber pressure is one part of a hyperbaric dose. Breathing gas, session length, frequency, and total sessions matter too, and a qualified clinician determines those for each individual. Higher pressure is not automatically better, and lower pressure is not automatically safer.
