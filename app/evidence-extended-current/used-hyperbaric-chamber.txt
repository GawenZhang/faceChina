L8: # Used Hyperbaric Chambers: What to Check Before You Buy
L9: 
L10: A used hyperbaric chamber is still a pressurized medical device, so its history matters. Before buying one, confirm what it is, who made it, how it has been serviced, and who will support it. OxyHealth occasionally has factory-refurbished chambers available.
L11: ## Why a chamber's history matters
L12: 
L13: A hyperbaric chamber holds a person inside an environment pressurized above normal atmospheric pressure. Its seals, closures, windows, valves, and air supply all have to work as designed, every session. Wear, poor storage, and unauthorized repairs are not always visible.
L14: 
L15: FDA regulates hyperbaric chambers as 【2†Class II medical devices†www.accessdata.fda.gov】. That classification does not lapse when a chamber changes hands.
L16: ## A checklist for any used chamber
L17:   * Identity. Get the manufacturer, model name, and serial number, and confirm them with the manufacturer.
L18:   * Clearance. Confirm the model has FDA 510(k) clearance. See 【3†what FDA clearance means】.
L19:   * Service history. Ask for records of inspections, repairs, and part replacements, and who performed them.
L20:   * Condition of the pressure boundary. For soft-sided chambers, inspect the bladder, zipper or seal assemblies, and windows. For hard-sided chambers, ask for the vessel's documentation.
L21:   * Air supply. Check the compressor and filters, and ask when filters were last changed.
L22:   * Documents. The chamber should come with its instructions for use.
L23:   * Support. Ask the manufacturer whether it will service the unit and what coverage, if any, applies to a second owner.
