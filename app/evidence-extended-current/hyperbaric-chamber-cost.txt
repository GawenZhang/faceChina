L193: ### Why FDA 510(k) Clearance Matters for Your Investment
L194: 
L195: A lower-priced chamber may save money upfront, but if it lacks FDA 510(k) clearance, you could be compromising on safety. FDA 510(k) clearance means the FDA has reviewed the device and found it substantially equivalent to a legally marketed device. FDA registration simply means a manufacturer has notified the FDA that they exist. OxyHealth chambers are FDA 510(k) cleared as Class II medical devices, not just registered.
L196: Before purchasing any hyperbaric chamber, always ask the manufacturer for their FDA 510(k) clearance letter and ASME PVHO-1 GR-1 form with serial number.

L201: ## Hyperbaric Chamber Cost Questions
L202: 
L203: Answers to the most common questions about hyperbaric chamber pricing, financing, and total cost of ownership.
L204: ### How much does a home hyperbaric chamber cost?
L205: 
L206: Home hyperbaric chamber pricing depends on the model, size, shell type, and the compressor and accessories included. OxyHealth offers models ranging from compact portable chambers to larger hard-shell systems, so pricing is quoted by model. Contact OxyHealth for a quote or ask about flexible financing options.
L207: ### What affects the price of a hyperbaric chamber?
L208: 
L209: Four things shape price: the chamber type (portable soft-shell or hard-shell), its size and seating, the pressure system and certifications behind it, and what is included, such as the compressor, mattress, setup, training, and warranty. A chamber built to recognized pressure vessel standards and backed by long-term support is the more dependable purchase over time.
L210: ### How much does HBOT cost per session at a clinic?
L211: 
L212: The cost of a single hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) session at a clinic varies depending on location, session length, and whether a hard-shell or soft-shell chamber is used. Clinic session costs can add up significantly over time, which is why many individuals and practitioners choose to invest in their own chamber.
L213: ### Is owning a hyperbaric chamber cheaper than clinic visits?
L214: 
L215: Yes, owning a hyperbaric chamber is significantly more affordable over time. When you divide the one-time purchase price across years of daily use, the cost per session drops to a fraction of what clinics charge.

L219: ### Why do some hyperbaric chambers cost more than others?
L220: 
L221: Price differences come down to safety certifications (FDA 510(k) clearance vs. simple registration), build quality, materials, compressor engineering, and expected lifespan. L222: ### Is hyperbaric oxygen therapy covered by insurance?
L223: 
L224: Medicare, Medicaid, and private insurers cover hyperbaric oxygen therapy in specific clinical situations, and coverage varies by insurer. Coverage is tied to the uses the device is cleared for; ask your physician and your insurer for specific coverage details.
