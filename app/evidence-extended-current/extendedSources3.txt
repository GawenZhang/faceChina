L13: ## What Is a Portable Hyperbaric Chamber?
L14: A portable hyperbaric chamber is a soft-shell, inflatable pressurized enclosure that delivers mild hyperbaric oxygen therapy (mHBOT) at 1.3 atmospheres absolute (ATA). These chambers are constructed from medical-grade materials and are designed to be set up and operated in any standard room with a household electrical outlet.
L15: Unlike fixed clinical hard-shell chambers that require permanent installation in a medical facility, portable chambers can be assembled in minutes and moved between locations as needed.
L16: The fundamental difference between portable and fixed clinical chambers lies in construction and pressure capability. Portable chambers use a soft-shell, inflatable design that reaches 1.3 ATA (approximately 4 psi above normal atmospheric pressure), while clinical hard-shell chambers are built from rigid steel and can operate at pressures up to 4.0 ATA. Both types increase oxygen dissolution into blood plasma based on Henry's Law of physics, but they serve different use cases.
L17: Portable chambers are suited for home wellness protocols, clinic treatment rooms, and mobile applications, while clinical chambers are designed for hospital settings and FDA-cleared medical conditions that may require higher pressures.

L20: The Science
L21: ## How Portable Hyperbaric Chambers Work
L22: Portable hyperbaric chambers work by creating a pressurized environment at 1.3 ATA using a purpose-built air compressor. When the chamber is sealed and the compressor is activated, air is pumped into the soft-shell enclosure until the internal pressure reaches 1.3 atmospheres absolute, which is approximately 4 pounds per square inch above normal atmospheric pressure.
L23: At this elevated pressure, oxygen molecules decrease in size and dissolve more readily into blood plasma, increasing total oxygen delivery throughout the body.
L24: The inflatable design of a portable chamber is engineered from medical-grade materials that maintain structural integrity under pressure. Each OxyHealth portable chamber includes a dedicated compressor that has been specifically designed for that model, ensuring consistent pressurization and quiet operation.
L25: Users can enhance their sessions by pairing the chamber with an oxygen concentrator, which delivers concentrated oxygen (typically 90-95% purity) through a mask or cannula while inside the pressurized environment.
L26: A typical session in a portable hyperbaric chamber lasts 60 to 90 minutes. The pressurization cycle takes a few minutes at the start, followed by the treatment period at full pressure, and a gradual depressurization at the end. Many users read, watch videos, or rest comfortably during their sessions. The chamber operates on a standard household electrical outlet, requiring no special wiring or infrastructure modifications.

L39: ### FDA 510(k) Clearance
L40: 
L41: What it is:
L42: 
L43: The FDA 510(k) process requires manufacturers to submit a premarket notification demonstrating that their device is substantially equivalent to a legally marketed predicate device in terms of safety and effectiveness. The FDA reviews the submission and issues a formal clearance decision.
