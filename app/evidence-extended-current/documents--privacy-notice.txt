L18: Our Privacy Practices
L19: ## How We Handle Your Data
L20: 
L21: We are transparent about what data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have. Read through each section below for full details.
L22: ### Overview
L23: 
L24: OxyHealth LLC is committed to protecting your personal information. We do not use your data for marketing purposes unless you have explicitly opted in.
L25: 
L26: All data is stored in a highly secure Zoho data center located in Colorado, USA, ensuring your information remains protected under stringent security protocols.
L27: 
L28: We do not sell your personal data to third parties under any circumstances.
L29: ### Data Collection
L30: 
L31: We collect basic identifiers such as names and email addresses only when users voluntarily provide them. This may occur through website registration, app usage, or service requests.
L32: 
L33: OxyHealth does not collect geolocation data, biometric data, or any other sensitive personal data.
L34: ### Usage Purposes
L35: 
L36: Your personal information is used to enable account access, deliver services, respond to customer support inquiries, and collect feedback to improve our products and services.
L37: 
L38: Data use remains strictly limited to authorized purposes. We will never use your information beyond the scope for which it was provided.
L39: ### Storage and Security
L40: 
L41: All information transfers occur on secure servers with encryption applied to both data in transit and data at rest.
L42: 
L43: Employee access to personal information requires background checks, and we conduct periodic security audits to assess and strengthen our data protection measures.
L44: ### Third-Party Sharing
L45: 
L46: Service providers who assist with our business operations may access your data, but only under strict contractual obligations that limit how that data can be used.
L47: 
L48: Your data will never be shared for advertising purposes, used to create automated profiles, or provided for data enhancement services.
L49: ### Your Rights
L50: 
L51: You have the right to request access to your personal data, request corrections to inaccurate information, request erasure of your data, or request a transfer of your data to another provider.
L52: 
L53: For any privacy-related inquiries or to exercise your rights, please contact us at privacy@Oxyhealth.com.
L54: ### HIPAA Compliance
L55: 
L56: OxyHealth fully adheres to HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) regulations for the privacy and security of protected health information.
L57: 
L58: Our compliance program ensures that all health-related data is handled with the highest standards of care, meeting both federal privacy and security requirements.
