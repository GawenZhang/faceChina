L30: ## Where they differ
L31: 
L32: ### Split design
L33: 
L34: The Fortius 420 EXP is built in sections for simplified transport and setup. It is intended for sites where a one-piece chamber is difficult to bring in.
L35: 
L36: ### Length and weight
L37: 
L38: The Fortius 420 is 91 inches long and weighs 1,250 lbs. The Fortius 420 EXP is 96 inches in total length, with a 62 inch interior length, and weighs 1,400 lbs.
L39: ### What is the same
L40: 
L41: Both operate up to 4.0 ATA (44 psi), have a 42 inch interior diameter, use reinforced steel construction with powder coating, deliver oxygen through a hood assembly, and are installed and calibrated by OxyHealth certified technicians with training included.
L42: ## Which setting each one suits
L43: 
L44:   * Fortius 420: Clinics and practices with a straightforward delivery path for a one-piece chamber.
L45:   * Fortius 420 EXP: Clinics with narrow doorways, corridors, or elevators that call for a chamber delivered in sections.
