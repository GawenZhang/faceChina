Before you buy anywhere

FDA cleared is not FDA registered

The two terms sound alike and mean very different things. Knowing the difference protects you from any chamber, from any seller.

FDA 510(k) cleared

The OxyHealth standard

  • The specific device was submitted to and reviewed by the FDA
  • Found substantially equivalent to a legally marketed device
  • Hyperbaric chambers are Class II devices, product code CBF
  • The manufacturer can show you a 510(k) clearance letter

FDA registered

Not the same thing

  • A company has listed its establishment and devices with the FDA
  • Registration is not a review of the device
  • It does not mean the device is cleared
  • Ask for the 510(k) letter, not the registration number

Always ask for the FDA 510(k) clearance letter, and for a hard shell chamber, the ASME PVHO-1 GR-1 form. View OxyHealth FDA documentation.
